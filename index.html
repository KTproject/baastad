Why Båstad

Båstad is one of Sweden’s most beautiful seaside towns and a pearl for tennis and golf lovers. What makes it special is the combination: compact, scenic, social in season, and unusually rich in sport, sea, dining, spa and gardens for a town of its size.

Sport: tennis, golf & padel

Tennis

Båstad’s defining sport: clay courts, tennis weeks and an atmosphere that reaches from morning practice to evening drinks. Private coaching introductions may be possible by request.

Golf

Coastal golf around the Bjäre peninsula makes Båstad ideal for weekend trips that blend rounds, sea views and long lunches.

Padel

A secondary but relevant social sport in Båstad: easy to combine with a weekend stay and dining plans.

Recommended sport experiences

  • Tennis weekend — clay sessions, town walks, late dinners.
  • Golf weekend — peninsula rounds with seaside pauses.
  • Padel + lunch — social play followed by a curated table.
  • Spa recovery day after sport — reset the pace without leaving town.
  • Sea swim + slow breakfast — a classic Båstad rhythm.
