Small, scenic and unusually complete, Båstad brings together clay courts, coastal golf, sea air, spa days, gardens and some of Sweden’s most elegant summer energy.

Explore the edit

Why Båstad

Båstad combines rare summer contrasts in one compact place: tournament-level tennis culture, rounds across the Bjäre coast, harbour mornings, refined dining and calm afternoons in garden or spa settings.

Sport: tennis, golf & padel

Tennis

Nordic clay-court heritage, summer week atmosphere and serious play from first light onward.

Golf

Coastal courses around the peninsula shape weekends built on sea views, long rounds and unhurried lunches.

Padel

A social complement to tennis and golf, easy to pair with dining and spa plans in the same day.

Recommended sport experiences

  • Clay weekend in town — morning sessions, harbour walks and late seasonal tables.
  • Two-course coastal golf plan — one technical round, one scenic round, both with sea air.
  • Padel with a recovery afternoon — social play followed by spa, swim and slow dinner.
  • Tennis plus private coaching request — for guests seeking a more directed sport itinerary.
